Privacy Policy for Aerio

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Aerio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Aerio Patient Telemetry Station mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our app connects to Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) medical pulse oximeter sensors (AP-20) to monitor vital signs such as Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO₂), Pulse Rate (BPM), and Perfusion Index (PI). We also store patient assignment details (such as Name, Age, Gender, and Station ID) locally on your device and synchronize them securely with our clinical backend server.

2. Types of Data Collected

  • Vital Signs Telemetry: SpO₂, Pulse Rate, and Perfusion Index measured via connected BLE oximeter devices.
  • Patient Metadata: Patient names, age, gender, and assignment timestamps entered by authorized station users.
  • Station Credentials: Station username and authenticated session tokens.

3. Use of Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the patient monitoring service.
  • To allow medical station operators to monitor live vital signs and sync records to the backend.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or synchronization failures.

4. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. We use encrypted HTTPS APIs, local database encryption, and secure BLE communication protocols. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@aerio.health.
